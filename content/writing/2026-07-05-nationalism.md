Yesterday, I went to watch a fireworks show at a local park. The city of Doral planned an elaborate event to celebrate July 4th. Thousands showed up to take part in the festivities.

It was pretty cool. Like admiring the grandeur of a traditional church. Awe-inducing even though I disagree with the ideas behind it (which is nationalistic fervor).

I've been thinking about writing a piece on nationalism, but it seems that I'm struggling to reconcile all my ideas and bring them together into a single piece. So, I'll try to do it over several.

The short version of it all would probably just be: Nationalism is a ridiculous idea.

Until next time, ✌️.
