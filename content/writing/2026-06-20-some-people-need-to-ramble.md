I'll occasionally hang out at Panera Bread to conduct my work. It has a cafe-esque environment.

Today, I walk in and head to the back as usual. I sit down, take out my laptop, plug it in, and get to work. I pull out an air filter I had recently ordered for testing (for reasons that I will not get into right now).

The man next to me notices the air filter and asks me about it. We start some conversation and he finds his way into flow state. That's when the rambling begins. He starts telling me about H-BOT, then the FDA, then stem cell therapy, then the FDA again, then PRP, then the FDA for the 3rd time, then the LSAT, and finally the case of a local psychiatrist that had his license revoked. All this rambling (which lasted about 30 minutes for reference), with nothing but nods and affirmations as my input.

Towards the end, he started making some weird arguments that seemed to be in defense of that psychiatrist, almost as if it impacted him personally. I pushed back on some of his ideas, but I quickly came to realize how futile that effort was. I also came to realize how much time I had spent listening to this guy. So, I started distancing myself to kill the conversation, and we eventually made it to that point.

No hate to the guy at all (if any could be interpreted from this writing). He was just an interesting fella saying some interesting things.

As for the moral of the story, I don't think there is one. And that's okay.

Until next time, ✌️.
